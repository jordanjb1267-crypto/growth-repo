# No-Shame Discipline

**Product:** Tilawah Lock

## Angle

Discipline content for people who are tired of being told they lack discipline. The premise: the phone is engineered by large teams to be hard to put down, and needing a mechanism to counter that is ordinary, not a character defect.

## Formats

- Structure over willpower — why external mechanisms work
- Small, boring, repeatable habit content
- Naming the design problem honestly
- Setup and consistency tips

## Guardrails

- This campaign exists specifically to counterweight the shame register, so a shame-adjacent line here is a worse failure than elsewhere. `RED-RELIGIOUS-PRESSURE` firing on a draft in this campaign should be treated as a signal the whole draft needs rewriting, not a phrase needing softening.
- Never position the reader as weak, lazy, or failing.
- No religious authority; no ruling on what is owed.

## Auto-publish eligible

Content that stays on mechanism, structure, and habit, without religious persuasion.
