# Pray. Read. Then Unlock.

**Product:** Tilawah Lock · **Status:** core promise campaign

## Angle

The product's promise stated plainly and repeatedly, in different registers. Three steps, in order, with the unlock as the natural consequence rather than the reward.

## Formats

- The three steps as a demo, start to finish
- Each step on its own: what the lock looks like during Salah time, during reading, at unlock
- Setup walkthroughs
- Quiet before/after: the morning with and without the lock

## Guardrails

- The sequence is a product behavior, not an obligation being imposed on the reader. Describe what the app does.
- No framing that suggests the app makes someone a better Muslim. It manages a phone.
- Religious references in this campaign are near-unavoidable, so most drafts here will be approval-gated or template-rendered. Expect that; do not write around it by stripping the promise.

## Approval required

Effectively all copy carrying the full promise, unless rendered from an approved template.
