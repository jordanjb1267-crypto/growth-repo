# Quran Before Phone

**Product:** Tilawah Lock · **Status:** APPROVAL-GATED IN FULL

## Standing constraint

Every draft in this campaign references Quran reading directly and is therefore approval-gated by default. The route to autonomy is the approved-template library, not an exception to this rule.

## Angle

The ordering itself: reading first, phone second. Content is about the sequence and about how small a first session can be — a page, a few lines — rather than about how much the reader should be doing.

## Formats

- Consistency over volume
- What the lock looks like during a reading session
- Reducing the activation cost of starting
- Product walkthroughs of the reading gate

## Guardrails

- Never quantify what the reader should read. No target is the brand's target.
- No ayah or surah citation without founder approval.
- No spiritual outcome claims — not "you'll feel closer", not "your day will go better".
- Never imply the app confers religious merit.

## Review note

Expect `YEL-RELIGIOUS-REFERENCE` on essentially every draft. That is the gate doing its job.
