# Read Before You Scroll

**Product:** Tilawah Lock · **Status:** default campaign

## Angle

The phone is the first thing in your hand in the morning and the last thing at night. Tilawah Lock puts a small, chosen obstacle in front of the scroll: read first, then unlock. The campaign is about the mechanism and the calm it produces, not about what you have been doing wrong.

## Voice check

Every draft in this campaign should pass one test: **would this make someone feel structured, or would it make them feel caught?** If it is the second, rewrite it.

## Formats

- Focus Lock demos: the screen, the wait, the unlock
- The mechanism explained without persuasion
- Gentle habit content — small, achievable, repeatable
- Privacy-first framing: the app is not watching, it is holding a door

## Guardrails

- No guilt, no shame, no religious pressure. This is the campaign most at risk of drifting there because the topic invites it.
- No religious authority. Describe what the app does; never rule on what the reader owes.
- No guaranteed spiritual outcomes.
- No ayah, hadith, or scholarly citation without founder approval.

## Auto-publish path

Content that stays on the mechanism and avoids heavy religious persuasion can go Green directly. Content carrying religious framing goes through the approved-template library in `/templates/tilawah-lock/` or through the approval queue. Both are fine; neither is a shortcut around the other.
