# Gross Rate Is Not Profit

**Product:** Rig Receipts · **Status:** default campaign

## Angle

A load that pays $3,200 is not a load that made $3,200. Fuel, deadhead, tolls, the maintenance reserve nobody sets aside, and the tires that wear the same whether the rate was good or not. The campaign makes the gap between gross and net visible, one line item at a time.

## Why it works

Every owner-operator has taken a load that looked good and felt bad afterward without being able to say exactly why. This campaign gives them the arithmetic for that feeling.

## Formats

- Fictional load math walkthroughs: rate in, costs out, what's left
- Single-cost-line explainers (deadhead, maintenance reserve, tolls)
- Before/after receipt organization
- Cost-per-mile fundamentals

## Guardrails

- All load examples fictional or privacy-redacted, and labelled as such.
- No broker names.
- No savings, income, or rate guarantees. Show the math, do not promise the outcome.
- Not anti-broker. The point is that costs are invisible, not that someone is cheating you.

## Auto-publish eligible

Fictional load math, cost-per-mile education, single-cost explainers, product demos.

## Approval required

Anything using a real route or rate, any figure presented as an outcome ("saved $X"), any tax angle.
