# Receipt Chaos

**Product:** Rig Receipts

## Angle

The shoebox, the glovebox, the faded thermal paper, the receipt that went through the wash. Recognition-first content about the physical reality of tracking expenses from a truck, leading into the product as the obvious fix.

## Formats

- Recognition posts ("the receipt is in the door pocket, it is now beige")
- Organization tips that work without the app
- Product demos: capture, categorize, retrieve
- Quarter-end and year-end readiness prompts

## Guardrails

- Tax *readiness* is fine. Tax *advice* is not, and anything naming a form, a deduction, or the IRS is approval-gated.
- Never suggest the app guarantees an audit outcome or a deduction.
- Humor is allowed here and should stay on the situation, never on the reader.

## Auto-publish eligible

Recognition content, organization tips, product demos.

## Approval required

Anything mentioning taxes, the IRS, deductions, or write-offs.
