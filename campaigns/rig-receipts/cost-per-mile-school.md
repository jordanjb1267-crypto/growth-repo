# Cost Per Mile School

**Product:** Rig Receipts

## Angle

Teach cost per mile properly, in small pieces. Most drivers have heard the term and have never sat down and computed theirs. The campaign is patient, numerate, and assumes intelligence rather than knowledge.

## Formats

- One cost category per post: fixed vs variable, how to classify, where people get it wrong
- Worked fictional examples with the arithmetic shown
- Common CPM mistakes (forgetting the empty miles, annualizing wrong, ignoring the reserve)
- Product demos where the app does the computation

## Guardrails

- Education, not prescription. No "your CPM should be X."
- Fictional figures only.
- No implied income outcome.

## Auto-publish eligible

The whole campaign, provided figures are fictional and no outcome is claimed.
