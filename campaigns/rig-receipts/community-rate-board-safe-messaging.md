# Community Rate Board — Safe Messaging

**Product:** Rig Receipts · **Status:** APPROVAL-GATED IN FULL

## Standing constraint

Every item in this campaign requires founder approval before publishing, without exception. The spec states Community Rate Board messaging requires founder approval, and nothing in this file overrides that. There is no auto-publish path here.

## Why the caution

Rate transparency sits closest to the two positions the brand must not take: that Rig Receipts is a load board, and that Rig Receipts is primarily anti-broker. Both are one careless sentence away in this subject area.

## Permitted framing

- Historical, aggregate, and educational. What rate data is, how to read it, why averages mislead.
- The driver's own history as the primary reference point.

## Prohibited framing

- Anything implying booking, bidding, dispatching, or contacting a broker.
- Naming brokers.
- Presenting aggregate rate data as a prediction or a guarantee.
- "Know what to charge" style framing that reads as rate-setting advice.
