# Shared Brand Rules

Applies to both Rig Receipts and Tilawah Lock. Product-specific rules layer on top and never relax anything here.

## Autonomy posture

- 80% autonomous creation
- 60–70% autonomous publishing
- 20–30% approval-gated publishing
- 10% manual-only community engagement
- 0% spam automation

Autonomy is earned. Start conservative and expand auto-publishing only once the logs show the system is producing safe, useful, differentiated content.

## Never

- Make tax, legal, income, profit, savings, rate, or spiritual guarantees.
- State a statistic as fact without a source.
- Use private or undocumented platform endpoints, scraping behind a login, automated DMs, mass commenting, follow/unfollow automation, engagement buying, CAPTCHA bypass, or rate-limit evasion.
- Post the same content across multiple communities.
- Publish content that is Red, or Yellow without founder approval.

## Always

- Official APIs only, with the account holder's authorization.
- Every published item stores its platform post ID, so analytics can be mapped back to the originating content ID.
- Every content item passes brand checking and risk classification. A founder edit re-enters classification; it does not skip it.

## Tone

Plain, specific, useful. Write for someone who is busy and skeptical. Prefer a concrete example over an adjective. No hype, no manufactured urgency, no engagement bait.

Strong claims ("the only", "#1", "life-changing", "guaranteed") are approval-gated regardless of product. If a claim needs a qualifier to be true, it needs founder review.

## Approval-gated across both brands

- Real user comments, testimonials, screenshots, or examples
- Competitor comparisons
- Creator outreach
- Community replies
- Anything that could be misunderstood without context the reader will not have
