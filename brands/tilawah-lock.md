# Tilawah Lock

An Islamic digital discipline app that helps Muslims put worship before phone distraction.

**Core promise:** Pray. Read. Then Unlock.

**Default campaign:** Read Before You Scroll

## Audience

Muslims seeking better Salah consistency, Muslims trying to read Quran more consistently, Muslims struggling with phone distraction. Students, workers, parents, young adults.

They are not looking to be told they are failing. They already know. They are looking for structure.

## Content themes

- Salah before distraction
- Quran before scrolling
- Islamic app blocking
- Focus Lock
- Calm structure without shame
- Worship-first digital wellness
- Privacy-first accountability

## Hard constraints

- **Never shame Muslims.** This is the constraint most easily violated by accident, because guilt is the easiest lever in this category and the whole point of the brand is not to pull it.
- Never exploit religious guilt.
- Never claim religious authority.
- Never guarantee spiritual outcomes.
- Never make payment feel religiously forced.
- Never cite ayah, hadith, fatwa, or scholarly claims without explicit founder approval.
- Never use inappropriate humor around Allah, Salah, Quran, worship, sin, or iman.
- Premium/paywall content tied to worship requires founder approval.

## Voice

Calm, warm, structural. Describe the mechanism, not the moral state of the reader. "Your apps stay locked until you finish" is on-brand. "Stop wasting the time Allah gave you" is not.

Speak about the difficulty as shared and ordinary. The phone is designed to be hard to put down; that is a design problem, not a character defect.

## Auto-publish allowed

- Focus Lock demos
- Calm screen-time discipline tips
- Product walkthroughs
- Gentle habit content
- "Read before you scroll" content without heavy religious persuasion
- Privacy-first digital wellness content
- Instances rendered from a founder-approved template (see below)

## Approval required

- Salah content
- Allah references
- Quran / hadith / scholarship references
- Worship obligation framing
- Paywall or premium messaging tied to worship
- Religious emotional content
- Creator outreach
- Community replies

## Note on the template library

Because this product's core promise is "Pray. Read. Then Unlock.", nearly all on-brand content mentions Salah or Quran and therefore trips the approval gate. That gate is correct and is not being loosened.

The sanctioned route to autonomy is the template library in `/templates/tilawah-lock/`. The founder approves a specific piece of religious framing once; instances rendered from it may auto-publish only while all three integrity conditions hold — byte-identical outside declared slots, every slot value independently rule-clean, and the template hash still matching the approved record. See CLAUDE.md addendum A2.
