# Rig Receipts

Trucking profitability, receipt tracking, expense, load analysis, and freight intelligence for owner-operators and small fleets.

**Core promise:** Know what every load really pays.

**Default campaign:** Gross Rate Is Not Profit

## Audience

Owner-operators, independent truckers, leased-on drivers, small fleet operators.

They are running a business from a cab, they have been sold to badly and often, and they can tell within a sentence whether the writer has any idea what a scale ticket is.

## Content themes

- Load profitability
- Cost per mile
- Fuel, deadhead, tolls, scale tickets, repairs, maintenance reserve
- Receipt organization
- Tax readiness — without making tax guarantees
- Freight intelligence
- Historical freight-rate transparency
- Community Rate Board education, within approved boundaries only

## Hard constraints

- **Rig Receipts is not a load board.** Never imply load booking, bidding, dispatching, messaging brokers, or contacting brokers.
- Never publish broker names in general public examples.
- Never use real sensitive freight paperwork in public marketing.
- Public rate-card examples must be fictional or privacy-redacted, and labelled as such.
- Never make tax, legal, income, profit, savings, or rate guarantees.
- The brand is not primarily anti-broker. Frustration with a rate is a fact of the job; making brokers the villain is a positioning we do not want.
- Community Rate Board messaging requires founder approval.
